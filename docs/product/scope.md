# Product scope baseline

## Vision
A global-first digital-product marketplace and product factory helping developers, founders, and agencies start from vetted digital assets.

## Audiences
- Buyers: founders, developers, agencies, product teams.
- Creators/vendors: people with verified rights to distribute their work.
- Operators: staff responsible for trust, quality, support, and commerce.

## Launch-critical capabilities
Public catalogue/search; identity and account security; sandbox checkout; verified payment state, orders and refunds; license/entitlement issuance and secure downloads; product submission/review and admin controls; auditability, support, monitoring, backup/restore and legal/policy pages.

## Later-phase unless separately approved
Autonomous agent publication/customer communication; paid AI generation; vendor payout automation; cryptocurrency checkout, BNPL and other high-risk payment products; gamification, affiliates, white-label, mobile API and enterprise claims.

## Truth in product status
Never publish fake user counts, sales, testimonials, review scores, performance claims, vendor earnings, inventory counts, or availability claims. Mockups remain previews until behavior is implemented and verified.
